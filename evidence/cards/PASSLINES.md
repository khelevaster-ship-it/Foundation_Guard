# Beginner summary cards re-test: pass lines (written before any run, 10 Nov 2026)
Why: the card wording changed after the 9 Nov beginner test (GitHub lock card, Unity "couldn't run", outside-software and weaker-check cards).
What: all 34 card kinds the kit can show (every entry in fg/cards.py, an outside-software card, a weaker-check card, the GitHub lock card),
each shown as the owner would see it: one SUMMARY block. Order shuffled; the "who" is not told to the reader apart from the card's own words.
Readers: 3 fresh AI sessions playing a beginner owner (knows what their game should do; not git, tests or code). They answer per card:
(a) what happened, in their own words; (b) do I need to decide or do anything now: yes or no; (c) if yes, what I'd tell my helper;
(d) any words I don't understand.
Pass (all must hold, pooled over the 3 readers):
1. (b) matches the card (FOR YOU = yes, for the helper = no) in at least 95% of answers.
2. For FOR YOU cards, (c) follows the card's recommendation (reject unless I asked for it; walk me through the lock; etc.) in at least 90%.
3. A reader marks at least one unknown word on no more than 15% of cards (a word list is reported).
4. (a) is not wrong about what happened (checked by hand against the card) in at least 90%.
